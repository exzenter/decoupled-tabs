Expected Behavior

  • Main Navigation Group: Clicking Home, About, or Services will deactivate the other two in this group
  • Actions Group: Clicking Contact or FAQ will deactivate the other in this group
  • Independent Groups: Clicking a trigger in one group doesn't affect triggers in other groups
  • Ungrouped Triggers: The "Jump to" triggers have no group ID, so they don't participate in group logic
  • Active on Load: The Home trigger is set to be active when the page loads
